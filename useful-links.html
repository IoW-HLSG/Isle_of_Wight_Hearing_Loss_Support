Our group

  • Isle of Wight Hearing Loss Support on Facebook (opens in a new tab)Our latest news, and the easiest way to message us.

Local support on the island

  • Wight Sense (opens in a new tab)The Isle of Wight's free sensory support service for people with hearing loss, sight loss or both. It offers home assessments, advice and equipment loans.
  • Isle of Wight Tinnitus Support Group (opens in a new tab)A support group for islanders living with tinnitus and hyperacusis.
  • Sight for Wight (opens in a new tab)The island's charity for people with sight loss, which also runs Wight Sense. Useful if you have both hearing and sight loss.
  • Ability Dogs 4 Young People (opens in a new tab)An island charity that trains assistance dogs for disabled children and young people. Get in touch to ask whether they can help a young person with hearing loss.

Hearing tests and hearing aids

  • Isle of Wight NHS Trust: Audiology (opens in a new tab)Adult and children's hearing services, implantable devices and tinnitus care at St Mary's Hospital, Newport.
  • NHS: Hearing loss (opens in a new tab)Medical information on symptoms, causes and treatment.

Isle of Wight Council

  • Support with hearing and vision (opens in a new tab)The council's sensory service for adults with hearing loss, sight loss or both.
  • Service for deaf children and young people (opens in a new tab)Specialist teacher support for deaf children and young people from birth to 25, working with families, schools and colleges.
  • Isle of Wight Council (opens in a new tab)Local council services and information.

British Sign Language and interpreters

  • Interpreters Live (opens in a new tab)Free video calls with a BSL interpreter, so you can contact organisations such as the Isle of Wight NHS Trust.

National charities and information

  • Hearing Link Services (opens in a new tab)Support and services for people with hearing loss and their families, such as groups and lipreading classes. Part of Hearing Dogs for Deaf People.
  • Hearing Dogs for Deaf People (opens in a new tab)The charity that trains dogs to alert deaf and hard of hearing people to important sounds at home, such as the doorbell and smoke alarm.
  • RNID (opens in a new tab)The UK charity for people who are deaf, have hearing loss or tinnitus.
