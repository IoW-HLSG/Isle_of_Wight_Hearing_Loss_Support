Accessibility statement

This statement applies to the Isle of Wight Hearing Loss Support website. We want as many people as possible to be able to use it, including people with hearing loss, sight loss, mobility difficulties, dyslexia or other conditions.

How you can use this website

  • Nothing on this website depends on sound. It has no audio or video.
  • You can zoom in to 200% or more, or change your browser's text size, without losing content or functionality.
  • You can move through every page using only a keyboard. The Tab key moves between links, and the focus highlight is clearly visible.
  • A "Skip to main content" link appears when you press Tab on any page.
  • The pages use headings, landmarks and descriptive link text, so they work with screen readers.
  • The site works without JavaScript and uses no cookies, timeouts, flashing or moving content.
  • Text and background colours have strong contrast, and the site respects your device's reduced motion setting.

AbilityNet has free advice on making your device easier to use if you have a disability: My Computer My Way (opens in a new tab).

How accessible this website is

We aim to meet level AA of the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) version 2.2. We believe the site meets this standard, but we have not yet had an independent accessibility audit. This statement is based on our own checks.

Known limitations

  • Links lead to other websites, including our Facebook Page, which we do not control. Their accessibility is the responsibility of their owners.
  • The photograph on the home page is decorative, so it has no text description.

Feedback and requests for other formats

If you find a problem that is not listed here, or you need information from this website in a different format, such as large print or plain text, please let us know. You can email IoW_HLSG@outlook.com or send a message through our Facebook Page (opens in a new tab). Please tell us the web page address, what the problem is, and the format you need. We will aim to reply as soon as we can.

If you are not happy with our response

The Equality Advisory and Support Service (opens in a new tab) offers free, independent advice if you feel your complaint has not been resolved.

About this statement

This statement was prepared on 4 October 2026. We will review it at least once a year and whenever we change the website.
