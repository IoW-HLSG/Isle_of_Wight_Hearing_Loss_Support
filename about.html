About us

  • We are a safe, friendly space for islanders living with hearing loss or a related condition, and for their families, carers and the professionals who support them.
  • We share experiences, practical advice, emotional support and information about local services and events.
  • We aim to raise awareness, reduce isolation and help people live well with hearing loss. You are not alone.
  • We are an independent community group. We proudly support the national and local charities and organisations that help islanders and their families and carers.
