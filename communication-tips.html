Communication tips

Small changes make a big difference when you are talking with someone who has hearing loss.

  • Get their attention before you start speaking.
  • Face the person, in good light, so they can see your face and lips.
  • Speak clearly at a natural pace. Shouting does not help.
  • Reduce background noise by turning off the TV or moving somewhere quieter.
  • If you are not understood, rephrase rather than repeat the same words.
